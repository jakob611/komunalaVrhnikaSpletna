This is the product/necessity pass over **all AO01–AO51 groups**, not another claim that compilation or API success proves usability. The [functional inventory](ADMIN-OPERATIONS-2026-11-01.md) supplies detailed action and test evidence. This ledger asks what staff need to accomplish, whether the steps lead to that result, whether the feature earns its complexity, and which missing behavior is necessary now. Synthetic local data and controlled transports were used; this pass sent no real messages and changed no production records.

## Confirmed focused release fixes and evidence

`src/test/admin-operational-release.test.tsx` adds nine controlled UI regressions for stale erasure previews, erase failure, empty scopes, scope-note rendering, schedule refresh/failed reload/recovery, empty schedules, terminal campaign states, zero recipients and operations-only subscriber/topic access. Other operations/permission tests remain in the original inventory.

The subscriber change does not grant a new permission: the operations API already permits that read. The campaign screen now exposes the existing read-only workflow. Region management remains with content editors. It shows chosen topic labels so staff can distinguish, for example, a confirmed roads subscriber from a water-alert recipient.

## Decisions deliberately not invented by the implementation

- **Automatic retention and backup deletion:** the operator must choose policy, responsible people and required records. The manual erasure tool does not invent a number of days or promise removal from outside mailboxes/backups.
- **Actual notification recipient and sender/domain:** retain verified account settings until the owner chooses a real institutional destination. Do not manufacture a staff account or claim that a synthetic test email proves deliverability.
- **Generic resend of terminal failures:** not added before release. A failed/network result can be uncertain, and the provider's deduplication window is finite. Blindly restarting old work could send duplicates or resurrect a withdrawn/unsubscribed notice. The durable case remains available for handling; the campaign panel directs the owner to inspect message/provider state. A future explicit resend workflow needs defined delivery-receipt and duplicate-handling semantics.
- **Exact frozen-audience approval:** current behavior recomputes eligible recipients when queueing and says so. If the organization needs approval of an immutable recipient set, that is a distinct requirement; the present preview is not described as such an approval.
- **Historical schedule snapshot restore:** metadata/status can be inspected and refreshed, and pending plans cancelled. Automatically reopening or rescheduling an old immutable snapshot after conflicting edits would need an explicit compare/review workflow. It is not represented as an existing button.

These are product/operational choices, not excuses to describe unimplemented behavior as working. The existing supported paths remain usable without a new design system, a generic mail resend, or an invented retention policy.
